🎰 The Problem with Default AI

Most people use AI like a slot machine — type something in, hope for the best, get disappointed with mediocre results.

Why? Because AI is packaged for the average person. It's dumbed down, sycophantic, and designed to give you cheap dopamine hits that keep you coming back.

If you use AI the way it's designed by default, you'll get average results.

💼 The Solution: Treat AI as a Digital Employee

Instead of guessing, you program AI exactly how to work — just like you'd train an employee.

This requires 500-2000 word prompts that eliminate guesswork. The shorter the prompt, the more AI has to guess. The more it guesses, the more it pulls from mediocre methods all over the internet.

But here's the problem: What if you don't know how to do what you're trying to teach AI?

That's what this tool solves. You'll learn HOW to do something while building the prompt that teaches AI to help you do it.

Write Detailed Instructions

Deconstruct exactly how YOU do something and write it all out. Zero guesswork.

✅ Best for: Skills you already know well

Ask AI to Create a Guide

For well-known topics, have AI detail the best practices, then turn that into a prompt.

Example: "Give me a detailed guide on creating the most comprehensive customer avatar" → Turn into interview prompt

✅ Best for: Established processes with clear best practices
